I am a researcher in the **Nanomaterials Group at Charles University** working in surface science, electrocatalysis, and materials for hydrogen technologies.

My research focuses on how the **composition, morphology, and surface structure of platinum-based electrocatalysts** influence catalytic activity and stability. I am especially interested in nanostructured, bimetallic, and multimetallic catalyst systems for electrochemical energy-conversion applications.

My work combines **magnetron sputtering**, electrochemical measurements, and complementary structural and surface-sensitive characterization to investigate catalyst preparation, restructuring, degradation, and performance under operating conditions.

### Research interests

- Electrocatalysis for fuel cells and electrolyzers
- Platinum-based bimetallic and multimetallic catalysts
- Nanostructured catalyst design
- Magnetron sputtering and thin-film deposition
- Catalyst degradation and activity–stability relationships
- Surface composition and restructuring under electrochemical conditions
- Surface and materials characterization
