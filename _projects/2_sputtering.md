I use **magnetron sputtering and related physical-vapor-deposition approaches** to prepare model and nanostructured electrocatalysts.

By controlling deposition conditions, composition, and morphology, these systems provide a platform for investigating how nanoscale structure influences electrochemically active surface area, catalytic performance, and material stability.

My work includes platinum catalysts ranging from compact layers to dispersed nanoparticles, as well as bimetallic and multimetallic catalyst systems.
