Catalyst surfaces are dynamic and can restructure during electrochemical or reactive operation.

My research examines these changes using complementary electrochemical, structural, and surface-sensitive characterization methods.

Understanding how the surface composition of Pt-based alloys changes under reaction conditions can help explain both catalyst activation and degradation and can guide the design of more durable materials.
