My research investigates how the composition and structure of platinum-based electrocatalysts evolve under electrochemical operating conditions.

A central goal is to understand the relationship between **catalytic activity and long-term stability**, particularly in bimetallic and multimetallic materials relevant to fuel-cell and hydrogen technologies.

This includes studying degradation, elemental redistribution, surface restructuring, and the influence of catalyst composition on electrochemical performance.
